# YouTube Music guide

Written for the YouTube Music website and Android app, 2025 layout. Premium features are marked.

## Basics

YouTube Music has a **Home** feed, **Explore** (new releases, charts, moods), and your **Library**. The player bar at the bottom controls playback; open it for **Up Next**,
**Lyrics** and **Related**. Songs and music videos are both available, and you can switch between **Song** and **Video** at the top of the player.

## Do this

### Play a song or artist
Use the search icon, type the name, and tap a result. Tap an artist's **Shuffle** or **Radio** to play their songs and similar ones.

### Add a song to your library or a playlist
1. In the player, open the three-dot menu (or the song's menu in a list).
2. Choose **Save to library** (adds it to your library) or **Save to playlist** and pick a playlist.
3. For a new playlist: **Save to playlist, New playlist**, name it.

### Like or dislike
The thumbs-up in the player likes a song and adds it to **Liked music**. Likes train your recommendations; thumbs-down hides it from similar picks.

### Play next or add to the queue
Open the three-dot menu on a song: **Play next** (right after the current one) or **Add to queue** (at the end).

### Make a radio of similar songs
Menu on a song, **Start radio**, or tap **Radio** on an album or artist.

### Download for offline (Premium)
Open an album or playlist, tap the **Download** arrow. In the app, **Library, Downloads** lists them. Set quality in **Settings, Downloads and storage**. Downloads need to reconnect to the internet every 30 days.

### Play with the screen off or in the background (Premium)
Background play needs Premium on phones. On the website it works in a browser tab. The free tier shows ads and stops when you leave the app.

### Cast to a speaker or TV
Tap the **Cast** icon in the player and choose the device, or use your phone's media output switch.

### Use a sleep timer
In the app: profile picture, **Settings, Playback**, or the three-dot menu of the player, **Sleep timer**. Pick a time or the end of the track.

### Change audio quality
**Settings, Playback and restrictions, Audio quality on mobile networks / on Wi-Fi**, choose Low, Normal, High or Always High (Premium has the best quality).

### Import music you already own
Upload your own songs at music.youtube.com from the profile menu, **Upload music**. They appear in **Library, Songs** under the Uploads filter.

### Fix wrong recommendations
**Settings, Privacy and location, Manage your YouTube Music data** lets you pause or delete listening history, which resets suggestions.

### Control it from your phone's lock screen or a headset
Playback controls appear in the Android notification and lock screen like any media app; the headset's play, pause and skip buttons work the same way. The voice assistant can use these same controls.

## Shortcuts (website)

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| Space | Play / pause |
| J / L | Back / forward 10 seconds |
| Shift+N / Shift+P | Next / previous song |
| Up / Down | Volume |
| M | Mute |
| / | Search |
| Shift+? | Show all shortcuts |

## When it goes wrong

- **Music stops when the screen turns off:** needs Premium; on Android also remove battery restrictions for the app (Settings, Apps, YouTube Music, Battery, Unrestricted).
- **A song is greyed out or unavailable:** it is not licensed in your country or was removed. Search for another version or the video version.
- **Wrong song plays or the queue keeps adding songs:** turn off **Autoplay** (the infinity icon in Up Next) and clear the queue from its menu.
- **Downloads fail:** check storage space, sign in again, and make sure the phone connected to the internet in the last 30 days.
- **Lyrics missing:** not every song has them; the **Lyrics** tab appears only when available.
- **Plays on the wrong device:** pick the device from the Cast icon; check the account is signed in on only the devices you expect (Google Account, Security).
